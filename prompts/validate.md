# Commercial validation and experiments — v2.0

Start from current registry, feedback and cycle; retain relevant customer research, competition, pricing and offer methods from MARKETING_SKILLS.md.

1. Define one segment, its latest observed attempt, desired outcome and the problem independently from the proposed product.
2. Reopen original evidence. Aim for >=3 independent behavioral sources for a passed evidence gate (5+ when feasible). A vendor offer, generic emotional complaint or proxy spending alone is not direct demand. More sources are not always better than one concrete paid test.
3. Map direct, indirect, human/manual and do-nothing alternatives. Track both customer successes and failures. A competitor feature page alone cannot establish that the need is solved. Research failed products and why people abandoned them.
4. Obtain actual user founder feedback. Only the user supplies founder_pull; store missing as null. Record appeal, aversion, willingness to work with this audience and daily work implications.
5. Specify what useful AI change is feasible now, and its failure mode. Do not equate a chat interface with differentiation. Keep an initially non-AI concierge test if it tests the actual demand.
6. Check setup, behavior change, time to first value, trust, recurring use and distribution. A list of businesses with products is potential fit, not demonstrated buying intent. Keep these distinct in prospect records.
7. Define test price and cost separately. Current adjacent spending is a proxy. Revenue-share commitment is partnership evidence, not proof that buyers pay. No invented revenue or market-size calculations.
8. Choose one riskiest uncertainty and the cheapest test: past-behavior interviews, manual service, narrow prototype, community trial or paid pilot. Unknown commercial gates can justify VALIDATE; they do not all need to pass before an experiment.
9. Create a complete experiment JSON + kit: hypothesis, audience, recruitment route, sample/offer, attempts, budget breakdown <=€500, success/failure/inconclusive thresholds, results ledger, owner/next action and next decision. At most one active experiment. Prepared is not active; active is not completed.
10. B2C tests require meaningful use, repeat behavior and a defined denominator/window, not signups alone. B2B/service tests seek payment or an explicit commercial commitment; distinguish paid from unpaid. Poor response to outreach can mean a channel/offer failure rather than no problem.
11. Capture actual outcomes, dates, costs, negative feedback and source refs. Never turn a planned call, mock interview or synthetic test into customer evidence. Spending, sending and using another person's content follow the actual user authorization; planning is not blanket permission.
12. Mark gates pass/fail/unknown with a rationale and evidence IDs: human_importance, founder_pull, ai_leverage, evidence, alternative_failure, behavior, distribution, monetization, disconfirmation. A pass requires evidence. Unknown is not a failed opportunity.
13. Use config/scoring.json only; no totals for incomplete/version-mismatched assessments. Provide per-dimension rationale/evidence. Score tiers never automatically promote a record. Record the decision and what would reverse it.
14. Update the same candidate ID and experiment; validate and regenerate state/views; commit atomically. After the test choose continue, change solution/offer/channel, watch or reject scoped to evidence. Do not add another layer of desk research when the missing information requires a real user.
